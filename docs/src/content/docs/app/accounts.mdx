## The Accounts tab

From the top:

- **Net worth**, and how much it's moved this month. Tap it for the [Net worth](/app/networth/) screen.
- **Where your money is**, a chart of bank, cash, wallet and investment balances.
- Your accounts, grouped into **Bank**, **Cash**, **Wallets**, **Investments**, **Credit cards** and **Loans**.
- **You owe › All debts**, which opens the [Debt](/app/planning/#debt) screen.
- **Insights**: **Cashflow**, **In and out by account**, **Spending by category**, **Coming up** and **Unusual activity**. Rupee households also see **Tax** here.
- **Archived** accounts, if you have any.

To add one, tap **+** and choose **Bank, cash or wallet**, **Investment**, **Credit card** or **Loan**.

## Bank, cash and wallet accounts

<Steps>
1. Tap **+ › Bank, cash or wallet**.
2. Choose the **Type**, and enter a **Name** and the **Bank**.
3. For a bank account, you can add the **Last 4 digits**, the **Account type** and the IFSC.
4. Enter the **Opening balance** and the date it was correct **As of**. Entries after that date adjust the balance.
5. Tap **Save**.
</Steps>

Leave **Count in safe to spend** on for accounts you spend from. Turn it off for money you don't want counted in [free to spend](/app/home/#free-to-spend), such as an emergency fund.

Tap an account to see its balance and its latest transactions. To change it, tap **Edit**. **Archive account** hides an account you no longer use from totals while keeping its history.

## Investments

Choose **+ › Investment** for things like mutual funds or shares that you track as one value. Enter the **Platform** and the **Current value**. Update the value whenever you like; net worth uses the latest one. Investments are shown as estimated values, and they don't count towards free to spend.

## Credit cards

<Steps>
1. Tap **+ › Credit card**.
2. Enter a **Name**, the **Bank**, the **Network** and the **Last 4 digits**.
3. Enter the **Credit limit** and what you owe on the card today.
4. Set the day the statement is made and how many days later it's due.
5. Choose the bank account you usually pay it from, then tap **Save**.
</Steps>

Record card spending in [Quick Add](/app/adding/) by choosing the card as the account.

### The card screen

The top shows **Owed on this card** and how much of your limit you're using. Under **This month's bill** you'll see the **Statement** total, **Pay at least** (the minimum due), the **Due date** and anything **Already paid**.

On this iPhone, Coffernest makes each statement on the statement day from what you've recorded on the card. To enter a statement from your bank yourself, tap **+ › Add statement** and fill in the dates, **Total due**, **Minimum due**, **Interest charged** and **Fees**.

Further down are **By month** (spent against paid, with interest and fees for the financial year), past **Statements** and their status, and the card's details.

### Record a payment

<Steps>
1. On the card, tap **Record payment of** the amount due.
2. Choose **Full**, **Minimum** or type an **Other amount**.
3. Choose the account it was paid **From** and the date, then tap **Record**.
</Steps>

This writes the payment in your books and moves the statement to paid, part paid or minimum paid. Coffernest doesn't pay the card; do that in your bank's app. You can also record a card payment as a [transfer](/app/adding/#move-money-between-your-accounts) to the card.

### Card EMIs

For a purchase converted to EMIs, or a loan taken on the card:

<Steps>
1. On the card, tap **+ › Add EMI**.
2. Choose **Purchase on EMI** or **Loan on card**, and say what it was for.
3. Enter the **Amount**, the yearly interest rate, the number of months, the **First EMI** date and any **Processing fee**.
4. Tap **Save**.
</Steps>

Each one appears under **EMIs on this card** with how many are paid and what's left. Swipe left to remove one.

## Loans

<Steps>
1. Tap **+ › Loan**.
2. Enter a **Name**, choose the **Type** (Home, Car, Personal, Education, Gold or Other) and enter the **Lender**.
3. Enter the **Amount borrowed**, the **Interest rate**, whether it's **Floating** or **Fixed**, the tenure and the **First EMI** date.
4. If your bank's EMI differs from what Coffernest works out, enter it in **EMI (if different)**.
5. Choose the account the EMI is paid from. Leave **Add EMI to bills** on so each EMI is expected and counted in free to spend.
6. Tap **Save**.
</Steps>

### The loan screen

The top shows what's **Still to repay**, how long is left, the EMI and rate, and how much of the loan you've repaid.

**Schedule** charts principal and interest over the life of the loan, with **Interest you'll still pay**, the **Next EMI** and when the loan ends. **Full schedule** lists every EMI with its principal, interest and the balance after it.

### Prepayments

When you pay a lump sum towards the loan, tap **Record prepayment**. Enter the amount and date, choose whether to **Reduce tenure** or **Reduce EMI**, and pick the account it was paid from. The schedule is worked out again from that date.

To see what a prepayment would save before you make it, tap **What if I pay extra?**. See [Debt and planning](/app/planning/#prepayment-simulator).

### Rate changes

For a floating-rate loan, record each change from your lender: tap **Add rate change**, enter the date it applies **From** and the **New rate**. Leave **Keep EMI the same** on if your lender changes the tenure rather than the EMI.

### A loan billed on a card

Some loans are really EMIs on a credit card. Tap **Billed on a credit card? Move it to the card** and choose the card. The loan becomes an EMI on that card with the same EMIs left, its bank debits stop, and the loan closes.

<Aside type="note">
Loan and card figures are worked out from the details you enter. Check them against your lender's statement if they matter for a decision.
</Aside>
