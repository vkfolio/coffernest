Net worth is everything you own minus everything you owe, from the balances in your books.

<Shots items={[{ src: networth, caption: 'Net worth' }]} />

## Where to find it

- On **Home**, tap the **Net worth** row near the bottom.
- In **Accounts**, tap the net worth at the top.
- In **Spending**, choose **In and out**, then **Net worth**.

## How it's worked out

| What you own | What you owe |
|---|---|
| Bank accounts | Loans |
| Cash | Credit cards |
| Wallets | |
| Investments | |

Each balance is the account's opening balance plus or minus every entry since. Loans count what's still to repay, and cards count what's owed on them today. Investments use the latest value you entered, so net worth is only as current as those values. When you have investments, Accounts notes "Includes estimated investments".

Property or anything else you'd like counted can go in as an investment account with its current value. See [Accounts, cards and loans](/app/accounts/#investments).

## The Net worth screen

The top shows your net worth today and how much it's gone up or down since last month.

### Month by month

The chart shows your net worth at the end of each month, and today for the current month. Choose how far back to look with **6M**, **1Y**, **3Y** or **5Y**. Touch the chart to see a month's figure and how it compares with the start of the range.

Tap **Compare what you own and owe** to add assets and amounts owed to the chart as separate lines. If some months are estimates, they're drawn dashed.

History starts from the earliest opening date among your accounts, so a new household has only a point or two. "Net worth history builds as you use the app" until there's enough to draw.

To see the figures as a list, tap the table button at the top right. **Net worth by month** lists each month's net worth with its assets and what was owed.

### What you own and what you owe

Below the chart, **What you own** and **What you owe** break the total down by kind, with each one's share. Tap a row, such as **Bank** or **Loans**, to see those accounts.

### Ratios

| Ratio | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Liquid and Invested | How much of what you have is in bank, cash and wallets, and how much is invested |
| Savings rate | The share of income you didn't spend or pay towards debt |
| Emergency cover | How many months of normal spending your liquid balance would cover, based on the last three months |
| Principal repaid | How much loan principal you've paid off this financial year, including prepayments |

Tap the **i** next to a ratio for a one-line explanation. Some ratios appear only once there's enough history to work them out.

<Aside type="note">
Net worth uses only what you've entered. It doesn't value property, gold or investments on its own, so keep investment values up to date if you want the figure to be close.
</Aside>
