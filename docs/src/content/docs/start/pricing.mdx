Coffernest is free to download. When you start your own books, you get 21 days free, then you can unlock Coffernest with a one-time purchase. There's no subscription and nothing to cancel.

<Shots items={[{ src: paywall, caption: 'Start the trial or unlock' }]} />

## The free trial

The trial is a free in-app item in the App Store called **21-day Trial**. Coffernest offers it as soon as you choose **Use on this iPhone**, or after you sign in to a server as its admin.

<Steps>
1. On the screen that opens, tap **Start 21-day free trial**.
2. Confirm with the App Store. You aren't charged.
</Steps>

The 21 days count from the moment you take the trial. Because it's an App Store item, it belongs to your Apple Account rather than to one iPhone, and you can take it only once.

You need to start the trial (or unlock) before you can add entries. If you tap **Not now**, Home shows a reminder you can tap to come back to it, and so does the **Coffernest** row at the top of **Settings**. While the trial runs, that row shows how many days are left. In the last three days, a note on Home reminds you too.

## The one-time unlock

**Unlock Coffernest** lets you keep adding to and changing your books for good. You pay once and keep it, including on a new iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account.

You can unlock at any time, during the trial or after it, from the same screen. The price is set by the App Store for your country and shown on the button before you buy.

## After the trial, without the unlock

Your books become read-only. Nothing is deleted or hidden.

You can still:

- see every screen and every entry
- export your books from **Settings › Export CSV**
- erase or delete everything
- sign in and out, and read notifications

You can't add, edit or delete entries, or sync with a server, until you unlock. If you try, Coffernest opens the unlock screen.

## Who doesn't pay

Sample data is always free. Choose **Look around with sample data** on the first screen to try everything without starting the trial.

On a Coffernest server, only the household's admin pays. The family members the admin adds use Coffernest free, with their own sign-in, and they don't see the trial at all. If the admin's trial ends without the unlock, the admin can't change entries until they unlock, but the family members they added can carry on.

<Aside type="note">
People you tag on entries, such as children, don't need a login, so they don't need the app at all. See [Spending](/app/spending/#people).
</Aside>

## Restore a purchase

If you've already unlocked or taken the trial with your Apple Account, for example on an older iPhone, Coffernest normally picks this up by itself.

If it doesn't:

<Steps>
1. Open **Settings** and tap the **Coffernest** row at the top.
2. Tap **Restore purchase**.
</Steps>

If nothing is found, Coffernest says "No earlier purchase was found for this Apple Account." Check that you're signed in to the App Store with the account you bought with.

## Refunds

Apple handles all payments for Coffernest, so refunds go through Apple. You can ask for one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com), signed in with the Apple Account you paid with.

If a purchase is waiting for approval, for example through Ask to Buy, Coffernest says so and unlocks as soon as it's approved.
