# Coffernest support

E-mail me@vkfolio.com and we'll reply within a few days. For bugs and ideas you can also open an
issue at https://github.com/vkfolio/coffernest/issues.

## Common questions

- **Restore my unlock on a new iPhone**: open the paywall (Settings → Plan) and tap Restore
  purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.
- **Refunds**: purchases are made through Apple. Ask for a refund at
  https://reportaproblem.apple.com.
- **What happens after the trial**: adding, editing and syncing entries needs the one-time
  unlock. You can still see, export and delete everything.
- **Delete my account or data**: on a server, Settings → Profile → Delete account. On this
  iPhone, Settings → Erase all data.
- **Run your own server**: see
  https://github.com/vkfolio/coffernest/blob/main/SELF_HOSTING.md.

Coffernest records what you enter. It never connects to your bank or moves money.
